Before submitting, we go through the checklist in docs/guidelines.md. The site then stays online, unchanged, until the results on 26 January 2027.

Rules we never break

  • Every match says what's similar, how similar, and where it breaks down.
  • Every number comes from a named dataset. Missing data stays missing; we never guess.
  • NASA data does the main work.
  • We check the method on known analog sites first: Atacama, Haughton Crater, the Dry Valleys.

How we're judged

Impact, Creativity, Validity, Relevance and Presentation, each scored 1 to 5.

Most of the score comes from the project page. The 30‑second demo mainly lifts Presentation.

Aiming for Best Use of Science, Best Use of Data and Best Mission Concept.
